## About Me

I build the data foundations that make AI possible.

I’m a Principal Data Architect with more than 16 years of experience helping enterprises design and scale modern data platforms, cloud architectures, and AI-ready data ecosystems.

My work spans data engineering, distributed computing, enterprise architecture, cloud transformation, and strategic AI enablement. I help organizations turn complex data environments into dependable, secure, and high-performing foundations for analytics and GenAI adoption.

**Currently, I help drive Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy for Data and AI.** I focus on translating complex technical capabilities into scalable, revenue-generating business solutions for enterprise clients.

I partner with business and technology leaders to translate technical capability into measurable commercial value—bridging data strategy, platform design, and operational execution across modern enterprise environments.

**Strategic Leadership & C-Suite Advisory:** My focus extends beyond technical execution to delivering measurable enterprise business value. I partner with C-suite and VP-level stakeholders to navigate AI governance, maximize ROI, design scalable operating models, and manage data/AI risk. By bridging the gap between complex data engineering and strategic business goals, I ensure that data platforms actively drive revenue, operational efficiency, and competitive advantage.

**Core expertise:** Data Architecture | Data Engineering | Cloud Platforms | Apache Spark | PySpark | AWS | Azure | GCP | Databricks | Snowflake | Microsoft Fabric | Data Migration | Generative AI | AI-ready Data Platforms
